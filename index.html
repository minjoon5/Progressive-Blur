Once upon a time there was a little prince who lived on a planet that was scarcely any bigger than himself, and who had need of a sheep. If you come at four o'clock in the afternoon, then at three o'clock I shall begin to be happy. As time draws near, I shall feel more and more happy. When the mystery is too overpowering, one dare not disobey. Absurd as it might seem to me, a thousand miles from any human habitation and in danger of death, I took out of my pocket a sheet of paper and my fountain pen. But then I remembered how my studies had been concentrated on geography, history, arithmetic, and grammar, and I told the little chap as old as I was that I did not know how to draw. He answered me, that doesn't matter, draw me a sheep. But if you tame me, then we shall need each other. To me, you will be unique in all the world. To you, I shall be unique in all the world. One runs the risk of weeping a little, if one lets himself be tamed. What is essential is invisible to the eye. It is the time you have wasted for your rose that makes your rose so important. You become responsible, forever, for what you have tamed. You are responsible for your rose. Men have forgotten this truth, but you must not forget it. All men have stars, but they are not the same things for different people. For some, who are travelers, the stars are guides. For others they are no more than little lights in the sky. For others, who are scholars, they are problems. But all these stars are silent. You alone will have stars as no one else has them. In one of the stars I shall be living. In one of them I shall be laughing. And so it will be as if all the stars were laughing, when you look at the sky at night. And when your sorrow is comforted, time soothes all sorrows, you will be content that you have known me. You will always be my friend. You will want to laugh with me. And you will sometimes open your window, so, for that pleasure, and your friends will be properly astonished to see you laughing as you look up at the sky. Then you will say to them, yes, the stars always make me laugh. And they will think you are crazy. It will be a very shabby trick that I shall have played on you. It will be as if, in place of the stars, I had given you a great number of little bells that knew how to laugh. And he laughed again. Then he became serious once more. This night, you know, do not come. I shall not leave you, I said. I shall look as if I were suffering. I shall look a little as if I were dying. It is like that. Do not come to see that, it is not worth the trouble. I shall not leave you. But he was anxious. I am telling you this, he said, also because of the fox. You become responsible, forever, for what you have tamed. You are responsible for your rose. I shall not leave you. There was nothing more but a yellow flash close to his ankle. He remained motionless for an instant. He did not cry out. He fell gently, the way a tree falls. There was not even any sound, because of the sand. And now, six years have already gone by. I have never yet told this story. The companions who met me on my return were well content to see me alive. I was sad, but I told them, I am tired. Now my sorrow is comforted a little. That is to say, not entirely. But I know well that he did get back to his planet, because, so shortly after daybreak, I did not find his body. It was not such a heavy body. And at night I love to listen to the stars. It is like five hundred million little bells. But there is one extraordinary thing. When I drew the muzzle for the little prince, I forgot to add the leather strap. He will never have been able to fasten it on his sheep. So now I keep asking myself, what has happened on his planet? Perhaps the sheep has eaten the flower. At one time I say to myself, surely not! The little prince puts his flower under her glass globe every night, and he watches over his sheep very carefully. Then I am happy. And there is sweetness in the laughter of all the stars. But at another time I say to myself, at some time or other one is absentminded, and that is enough! He forgot the glass globe some night, or perhaps the sheep got out without making any noise, in the night. Then the little bells are all changed into tears! Here, then, is a great mystery. For you who also love the little prince, and for me, nothing in the universe can be the same if somewhere, we do not know where, a sheep that we never saw has, yes or no, eaten a rose. Look up at the sky. Ask yourselves, is it yes or no? Has the sheep eaten the flower? And you will see how everything changes. And no grown-up will ever understand that this is a matter of so much importance! This is, to me, the most beautiful and most saddest landscape in the world. It is the same landscape that you have seen before, but I have drawn it once again to be sure that you see it clearly. It is here that the little prince appeared on Earth, and then disappeared. Look at it carefully so that you will be sure to recognize it, if you should travel some day to the African desert. And, if you should happen to pass by this place, please do not hurry. Wait just a little while just under the star! If then a child comes to you, if he laughs, if he has golden hair, if he refuses to answer your questions, you will easily guess who he is. Then be kind! Do not leave me in my sadness. Write to me quickly that he has come back.
